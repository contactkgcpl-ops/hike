Common queries about our fully automated ctc tea processing plant plant consultancy services.

Entry-level plants typically start at 500 kg/day of finished fully automated ctc tea processing plant, with medium-scale operations ranging from 1–5 MT/day. We design capacity based on raw material availability and market demand in your region.

The key steps are: receiving & washing, peeling, slicing, blanching (to prevent enzymatic browning), drying (drum/spray/cabinet), milling to fine powder, sieving, and moisture-proof packaging. Each step is critical for product quality.

fully automated ctc tea processing plant is used extensively in snack manufacturing (chips, kurkure), instant soup & gravy mixes, bakery products, baby food, and as a thickening agent in food processing. Export markets in the Middle East and Europe also offer strong demand.

For domestic sales, FSSAI license is mandatory. For export, you will need EIC/APEDA certification, HACCP, ISO 22000, and country-specific import approvals. We assist with all certifications end-to-end.
