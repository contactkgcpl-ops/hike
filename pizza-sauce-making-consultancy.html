Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about our pizza sauce making plant consultancy.

Primary raw materials include fresh tomatoes or tomato paste/puree, olive oil, garlic, herbs (oregano, basil), salt, sugar, and citric acid. We guide you on procurement strategies including seasonal sourcing, contract farming, and using tomato paste concentrate as a year-round alternative.

With proper hot-fill or retort processing, commercially produced pizza sauce can achieve 12-24 months shelf life at ambient temperature. Refrigerated variants with natural preservatives can achieve 6-12 months. We guide you on selecting the right preservation technology for your target market.

Yes, a well-designed pizza sauce plant can be easily adapted to produce pasta sauce, marinara, arrabiata, and other tomato-based sauces with minimal equipment changes. We help you plan a multi-product facility to maximize asset utilization and revenue streams.

QSR and HORECA customers typically prefer bulk packaging — 5 kg/10 kg pouches, 3-5 L containers, or bulk drums. Retail customers prefer glass jars (200g-700g) or PET bottles. We help you design a multi-format packaging line to serve both channels effectively.
