Contract Manufacturing & Co-Packing

Connect with verified third-party manufacturers, white-label packers, and private-label partners to scale production without heavy CapEx.

Asset-Light Scaling for Food & Beverage Brands

Building your own production plant is not the only way to scale. In fact, many of the world's most successful food brands leverage contract manufacturing to remain agile, lower their risk, and scale production rapidly. Finding a facility that is compliant, reliable, and technically capable can be difficult.

Hike’s Contract Manufacturing network connects you with pre-audited co-packers, third-party manufacturers, and private-label units across India. We match your product requirements (stabilization, packaging form, production throughput) with plants that run compatible lines.

We oversee factory audits, review raw material sourcing controls, negotiate Service Level Agreements (SLAs), and coordinate trial batches. This guarantees that your outsourced production meets the exact same quality and safety standards as your proprietary benchtop formulations.

Our Methodology

We manage the complete transition. From technology transfer (sharing formulations and process steps under NDAs) and setting up inline Quality Assurance (QA) checkpoints, to verifying packaging run efficiencies — we ensure your manufacturing partner performs consistently.

Key Deliverables

  • Third-Party Manufacturer Matching
  • White-Label & Private-Label Setup
  • Co-Packer Sourcing & Capability Assessment
  • Facility Audits (GMP, Hygiene, Capacity Checks)
  • Contract Drafting & SLA Negotiation
  • Inline Production Monitoring Setup
  • Quality Assurance & Tech-Transfer Support
