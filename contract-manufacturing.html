Sourcing & Procurement

Vetted ingredient sourcing, supplier audits, price negotiations, and structured supply agreements to reduce procurement lead times.

Securing High-Quality, Resilient Raw Material Supply

At Hike, we believe that procuring high-quality ingredients at stable pricing is critical to food manufacturing. Our Sourcing & Procurement services connect brands with vetted raw material suppliers and co-packers.

Our dedicated team of procurement consultants, vendor auditors, and contract specialists work side-by-side with you. We identify reliable ingredient suppliers, audit vendor facility hygiene, negotiate bulk pricing, and establish backup supply lines.

We analyze commodity pricing, structure B2B purchase agreements, and audit vendor quality standards to minimize lead times and protect your supply chains from raw material risk.

Our Sourcing Methodology

We follow a strict, phase-gated approach: supplier mapping, quality testing, vendor compliance audits, price negotiation, and supply agreement structuring. This minimizes material risk and guarantees cost optimization.

Key Deliverables

  • Raw Material & Ingredient Sourcing
  • Supplier Facility & Hygiene Audits
  • B2B Price Negotiations & Cost Control
  • Vendor Evaluation & Scoring Systems
  • Supply & Purchase Agreements Setup
  • Backup Supplier Identification
  • Procurement Lead Time Reduction
