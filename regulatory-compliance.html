Logistics & Distribution

Route optimization, third-party logistics (3PL) selection, freight planning, and last-mile delivery services across India.

Streamlining B2B Logistics & Delivery Networks

At Hike, we believe that an efficient logistics network is critical to scaling your brand. Our Logistics & Distribution services are designed to lower transit costs and speed up transit times.

Our dedicated team of logistics experts, route designers, and carrier managers work side-by-side with you. We audit delivery routes, identify high-performance third-party logistics (3PL) partners, setup cross-docking hubs, and implement freight plans.

We analyze route layouts, manage fleet operations, and optimize last-mile deliveries to retail stores, ensuring your products are always delivered on schedule.

Our Logistics Methodology

We follow a phased approach: distribution audit, route optimization, carrier vetting, contract negotiation, and dispatch control setup. This ensures reliable B2B transport flow and lower freight expenses.

Key Deliverables

  • Route Optimization & Transit Audits
  • 3PL Partner Selection & Vetting
  • Primary & Secondary Freight Planning
  • Last-Mile Delivery Network Setup
  • Cross-Docking & Distribution Hubs
  • Fleet Operations & Tracking Setup
  • Reverse Logistics & Returns Planning
