Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about our cocoa powder processing plant consultancy.

Natural cocoa powder is produced without alkalization and retains a strong, acidic chocolate flavor. Dutch-process (alkalized) cocoa is treated with alkali to neutralize acidity, resulting in a milder, darker powder preferred for baking. We help you decide which type to produce based on your target market.

The primary raw material is raw or fermented cocoa beans sourced from producing regions like Ghana, Ivory Coast, Ecuador, or India. We provide guidance on bean quality assessment, sourcing strategies, and storage requirements.

A cocoa processing plant can produce cocoa powder (natural & alkalized), cocoa butter, cocoa liquor/mass, and cocoa nibs — all high-value B2B ingredients sold to chocolate, dairy, bakery, and beverage manufacturers globally.

Yes, the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) requires due diligence for cocoa exported to Europe. We guide exporters through traceability systems, geolocation documentation, and compliance frameworks required under EUDR.

Absolutely. We provide guidance on organic cocoa certification (USDA Organic, EU Organic, India Organic), fair trade certifications, and Rainforest Alliance standards that command premium pricing in global markets.
