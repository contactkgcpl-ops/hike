Common queries about our consultancy services.

A small flour mill with 5-10 MT/day capacity typically requires INR 25-60 lakhs including civil works, machinery, and working capital. We prepare accurate DPRs with detailed cost projections during the feasibility phase.

Power requirements vary by scale. A 10 MT/day flour mill typically needs 30-50 kW connected load. We conduct detailed load calculations and recommend backup power solutions as part of our plant design.
