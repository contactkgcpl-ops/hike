Product Placement Strategy

Securing eye-level retail shelf space, planogram designs, brand visibility audits, and retail partner negotiations across India.

Maximizing Shelf Visibility & Retail Sales Velocity

At Hike, we believe that getting your product on the retail shelf is just the beginning. Our Product Placement Strategy services are designed to secure high-visibility shelf space and drive consumer off-take.

Our dedicated team of retail category consultants, visual merchandisers, and brand specialists work side-by-side with you. We design optimal planograms, audit competitor shelf share, check point-of-sale displays, and implement in-store promotion strategies.

We analyze retail foot traffic, negotiate placement deals with supermarket category managers, and audit planogram compliance to maximize your brand's retail presence.

Our Placement Methodology

We follow a structured phase: retail category analysis, planogram layout mapping, shelf space allocation planning, retailer pitch presentation, and store visibility audits. This ensures maximum brand share of shelf.

  • Store Layout & Navigation Consulting
  • In-Store Merchandising & POS Displays
